Introduction

Welcome to Mingle. Mingle is a premium social networking platform created to connect individuals, families, creators, businesses and communities in a secure, respectful and inspiring digital environment. Your privacy is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how we protect it and the choices available to you. By creating an account or using Mingle, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Mingle, we may collect information including:

  • Name and profile information.
  • Email address.
  • Pi authentication details when you choose Pi Login.
  • Profile photo and cover photo.
  • Posts, comments and reactions.
  • Messages sent through Messenger.
  • Photos, videos and stories you upload.
  • Creator information.
  • Marketplace activity.
  • Business Page information.
  • Dating profile information.
  • Contact requests.
  • Device information.
  • Security logs.
  • Crash reports.
  • Network diagnostics.
  • Notification preferences.

How We Use Information

Your information helps us:

  • Operate Mingle safely.
  • Create and manage your account.
  • Improve platform performance.
  • Protect against fraud.
  • Provide AI-powered features.
  • Recommend relevant content.
  • Improve creator experiences.
  • Support businesses.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve platform security.
  • Develop new features.
