Mingle Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Mingle is committed to protecting your personal information while providing a secure and enjoyable social networking experience.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mingle. Mingle is a social networking platform where people connect, communicate, build communities, share ideas, create businesses and interact in a trusted online environment. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and disclose your information whenever you use any Mingle service. By creating a Mingle account or using any Mingle service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Depending on how you use Mingle, we may collect:

  • Pi Authentication Information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide messaging, social networking and marketplace services.
  • Improve the performance, security and reliability of Mingle.
  • Personalise your experience.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing Your Information

Mingle does not sell your personal information. We may share information only when necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain our services.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal processes.
  • Protect the rights, safety and security of Mingle and its users.
  • Provide services you have requested.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request a copy of your personal information where applicable.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards to help protect your information. While no online platform can guarantee absolute security, Mingle continually works to strengthen the protection of user accounts and data.
