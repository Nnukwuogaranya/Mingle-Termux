Mingle AI & Automated Systems Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Responsible use

Mingle may use AI and automated systems to assist support, moderation, safety, recommendations, translation, accessibility and platform operations.

Human oversight

Automated systems may make mistakes. Where appropriate, Mingle may provide human review, appeals or correction mechanisms.

Privacy

AI systems are operated subject to Mingle's Privacy Policy and applicable law. Mingle aims to limit unnecessary use of personal information.

Safety

Mingle may use automated detection to identify spam, fraud, harmful content or suspicious behaviour, subject to applicable law and platform policies.
