Mingle Anti-Scam & Fraud Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Zero tolerance for fraud

Mingle prohibits phishing, impersonation, fake investment schemes, fraudulent listings, payment deception, account theft, referral manipulation and other deceptive activity.

Protection measures

Mingle may use verification, automated systems, moderation, transaction monitoring, account restrictions and human review to reduce fraud.

Reporting

Users should report suspicious accounts, messages, listings or transactions through available Mingle reporting tools or Support.

Enforcement

Mingle may remove content, restrict transactions, suspend accounts, reverse fraudulent rewards and cooperate with lawful investigations.
