Who We Are

Mingle is a next-generation social networking platform built for the modern world. Our vision extends beyond social media. We are building a complete digital ecosystem where people can communicate, create, learn, build businesses, discover opportunities, support one another and form genuine relationships within one secure platform. We believe technology should bring people closer together rather than divide them. Every feature inside Mingle is designed with simplicity, elegance, trust and human connection in mind.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower individuals, creators, entrepreneurs, organisations and communities by providing a secure, intelligent and beautiful platform where ideas grow, businesses flourish and meaningful relationships are built every day.

Our Vision

To become one of the world's most trusted digital communities by combining social networking, artificial intelligence, digital commerce, creator empowerment and responsible innovation into one premium platform.

What Makes Mingle Different?

Mingle is more than a place to share posts. It is designed to become an intelligent digital community where communication, commerce, entertainment, learning and opportunity exist together. Our goal is to create a platform where every user can belong, every creator can grow, every business can reach customers and every community can thrive.

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is woven throughout the Mingle experience. Our AI systems help improve safety, detect harmful activity, recommend relevant content, assist customer support, translate conversations, enhance accessibility, support creators and improve the overall quality of the platform. Our goal is to use AI responsibly, transparently and in ways that respect user privacy and human dignity.

Pi Ecosystem

Mingle proudly embraces the Pi ecosystem and is designed to support Pi technologies where available. Within the Mingle platform, Pi Coin is intended to serve as the primary in-platform digital currency for eligible marketplace purchases, creator rewards, subscriptions, premium memberships, advertising services, tipping, peer-to-peer (P2P) activities and other supported transactions, subject to applicable laws and platform requirements.

Creator Economy

Creators are at the heart of Mingle. We are building powerful tools that help creators publish content, grow audiences, build communities, receive tips, earn rewards, sell digital products, collaborate with brands and develop sustainable online businesses. Our vision is to create an environment where creativity is recognised and rewarded.

Marketplace & Business

Mingle Marketplace enables individuals and businesses to discover customers, sell products, promote services and build trusted digital storefronts. Business Pages provide organisations with professional tools for communication, customer engagement, advertising and community building.

Messenger & Communication

Mingle Messenger is designed for fast, secure and meaningful communication. Users can connect through private messaging, group conversations, media sharing and future communication features designed to strengthen relationships while respecting privacy.

Dating

Mingle Dating is designed to help people build genuine and respectful relationships. Our focus is on authenticity, safety and meaningful connections, supported by privacy controls, reporting tools and intelligent safety systems.

Safety & Trust

Creating a safe community is one of our highest priorities. Mingle combines modern technology, responsible AI and community reporting tools to help reduce spam, scams, impersonation, fraud and harmful behaviour while encouraging respectful interactions.

Looking Ahead

Mingle is being built for the future. As the platform grows, we plan to introduce additional creator tools, AI-powered assistants, educational experiences, business services, digital commerce innovations, enhanced community features and technologies that continue to strengthen meaningful human connection across the world.

Our Promise

Everything we build is guided by one simple belief: Technology should help people connect, create, learn, grow and belong. Our commitment is to build Mingle with excellence, integrity, innovation and respect for every member of our global community.
