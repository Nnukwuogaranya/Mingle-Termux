Mingle Referral Rewards

Earn from the real value your direct referrals generate for Mingle.

1. Purpose of the Referral Programme

The Mingle Referral Programme is designed to reward members who introduce new users to Mingle and help the platform grow through genuine user activity.

Referral rewards are intended to recognise value that a direct referral actually generates for Mingle. The programme is not designed as a payment for simply registering users or recruiting people into a chain.

2. Direct Referrals Only

A member may earn referral rewards only from their qualifying direct referrals.

  • You earn from users directly referred by you.
  • You do not earn from another person's direct referrals.
  • You do not earn from activities performed by another member merely because that member appears somewhere in a referral tree.
  • Mingle does not operate a multi-level commission structure under this programme.

3. Revenue Must Actually Be Generated

A referral reward becomes applicable only when the direct referral performs a qualifying activity that generates actual revenue for Mingle.

Simply inviting someone, creating an account, logging in, viewing content or remaining inactive does not automatically create a monetary referral reward.

Simple principle: If a referral generates qualifying revenue for Mingle, the referring member may receive the applicable referral share. If no qualifying revenue is generated, there is no referral commission.

4. Referral Percentage

Mingle may establish, modify or discontinue the percentage or amount allocated to qualifying referral rewards.

The applicable rate may depend on the particular revenue-generating activity, product, service, campaign or programme in force at the relevant time.

Mingle will determine the applicable rate and may publish the applicable rate within the platform.

5. Mingle Retains the Majority of Revenue

Referral rewards are intended to represent only a portion of qualifying revenue. The majority of applicable revenue remains with Mingle to support platform operations, technology, security, infrastructure, development, customer support, compliance and future growth.

6. No Recruitment Commission

Mingle does not promise payment merely because a person recruits another person.

The programme is based on actual qualifying economic activity rather than recruitment volume. Members must not represent Mingle as a get-rich-quick programme, investment scheme, pyramid scheme or guaranteed income opportunity.

7. Earnings Records

Mingle may provide members with a referral dashboard showing information such as direct referrals, qualifying activity, generated revenue, applicable rewards and available balances.

Displayed earnings may be subject to verification, settlement, fraud checks, reversals, refunds, chargebacks, disputes or other adjustments.

8. Small Balances and Withdrawals

Mingle may allow members to withdraw eligible referral rewards without imposing a minimum withdrawal threshold.

Where supported by the applicable payment infrastructure, even a very small eligible balance may be available for withdrawal.

Withdrawal processing may nevertheless be subject to network fees, payment-provider limitations, verification requirements, technical limitations, applicable law and fraud-prevention controls.

9. Automatic Withdrawals

Where automatic withdrawals are enabled, Mingle may automatically transfer eligible referral rewards according to the withdrawal settings and payment method selected by the member.

Automatic withdrawal availability may vary according to the member's account type, supported currency, payment infrastructure, security requirements and applicable laws.

10. Pi Members

For members accessing the Pi-facing Mingle experience, eligible Mingle rewards may be denominated and settled in Pi where the applicable Pi integration and Mingle systems support such transactions.

Mingle will respect applicable requirements governing the use of Pi within the relevant Pi-facing experience.

Any conversion displayed by Mingle is a Mingle platform calculation or reference unless expressly stated otherwise. Mingle does not represent an internal conversion figure as an official Pi Network market valuation.

11. General Mingle Community

Members using the broader Mingle experience may receive eligible referral rewards through the currency or payment method made available to that part of the platform.

The availability of a particular currency, payment method or withdrawal mechanism may depend on location, account type, applicable law and Mingle's payment infrastructure.

12. Prohibited Referral Activities

Members must not attempt to manipulate the referral programme through:

  • Fake or duplicate accounts.
  • Self-referrals.
  • Automated or fraudulent registrations.
  • Purchased or fabricated traffic.
  • Bot activity.
  • Fraudulent transactions.
  • Spam invitations.
  • Misleading income claims.
  • Impersonation.
  • Attempts to exploit technical errors.
  • Any activity intended to artificially create referral revenue.

13. Fraud, Reversals and Adjustments

Mingle may investigate suspicious referral activity and may delay, adjust, reverse or cancel rewards associated with fraudulent, disputed, refunded, cancelled or otherwise invalid transactions.

Where necessary, Mingle may suspend referral earnings or related accounts while an investigation is conducted.

14. No Guaranteed Income

Participation in the Mingle Referral Programme does not guarantee any particular income, revenue or financial return.

Actual earnings depend on genuine qualifying activity and the revenue generated by a member's direct referrals.

15. Changes to the Programme

Mingle may modify, suspend or discontinue the Referral Programme, including its qualifying activities, reward rates, withdrawal methods and eligibility requirements.

Where appropriate, material changes may be communicated through the platform.

16. Contact

Questions regarding referral rewards may be directed to Mingle Support or the appropriate Mingle department.
