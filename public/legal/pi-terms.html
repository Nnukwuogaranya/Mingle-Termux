Mingle Pi Integration & User Terms

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Mingle remains Mingle

Mingle is a separate social platform. A Pi-related entrance provides an access method into Mingle and does not turn the entire platform into a separate Pi social network.

Pi transactions

For eligible users participating through supported Pi functionality, Mingle intends to use Pi as the designated in-platform currency for supported transactions within that Pi-access experience, subject to Pi Network requirements, technical availability and applicable law.

Pi Network requirements

Mingle will not intentionally represent unsupported functionality as official Pi Network functionality. Pi-related features remain subject to the applicable Pi ecosystem rules and technical requirements.

Conversion displays

Where Mingle provides a Pi-to-reference-currency converter, displayed values are platform information for supported transactions and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of external market value.
